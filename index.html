The path

Six steps. You approve two.

Nothing goes out as a finished reel until you have read the script and watched the cut. If a step needs a change, we fix that part and keep the rest.

  1. 01

    Share what the business does

    A short brief: the work you want more of, the area you serve, and how you like to sound. No film day required.

  2. 02

    A script in your voice

    The engine drafts a short-form script aimed at local customers, with a clear close and a simple next step.

  3. Gate 1 · You read and edit the script You can change lines, drop a claim, or tighten the close. Voice, captions, and on-screen text follow what you approve.
  4. 03

    Voice and the AI avatar

    The approved script is spoken and lip-synced to the AI avatar, either Marcelo’s presenter look or an avatar built for your business if we agree on that setup.

  5. 04

    Graphics and captions in the engine

    On-screen type, simple graphics, and captions are built in the reel engine so the frame matches what is being said.

  6. Gate 2 · You watch the reel Ask for a visual or caption change and we re-render that part. The script you already signed off stays put unless you want it opened again.
  7. 05

    You get the finished file

    A vertical reel, ready to post, plus the working files if you want them. You own what we delivered.

  8. 06

    A path from the reel to a conversation

    Every script can close on a simple comment, message, or call to action, so a view has somewhere to go besides a like.

From a view to a lead

A reel is not a booked job. So each one needs a way in.

The file alone is not the whole offer. The close of the reel, the reply in messages, and the handoff into a call or quote request are part of the same loop.

01 · In the reel

A clear next step

The script ends on something a local customer can actually do: comment a word, send a message, or ask for a quote. Not a vague “link in bio” and nothing else.

02 · In the messages

A reply that can wait for you

When we set this up, a comment or DM can trigger a saved reply with the next step, so a person who watches at night is not left hanging until you finish a job.

03 · In your calendar

A path to a real conversation

The reply can point to a booking link, a form, or a simple “text us the job” prompt. You choose the handoff that already fits how you take work.
